Earmark: Like a bookmark, but for listening later. Sign in with Google and your list syncs across every device.

I built this app as a personal utility.
I spend a lot of time of my day commuting to class, and with my daily schedule, there is barely enough time for me to sit and read articles.
I figured: what if I could just drop the things I want to read into a podcast format and stay updated on the go? 
That is how Earmark was formed.
Sure, similar tools exist out there, but I wanted to build this my way—kind of like cooking my own meals. 
It's a little something made just for me, and honestly? I love it already.
